# Worker guide

For the person walking the route with a phone. Your supervisor gives you a username and a password. You cannot change the password; if it stops working, ask them to set a new one.

## Before you go

- Open the app's address in your phone's browser (Chrome on Android, Safari on iPhone) and **sign in**.
- Allow **location** and **camera** when the phone asks.
- Charge the phone. Recording uses the screen, camera and GPS for a long time.
- Free some storage. A minute of video is about 30 MB.

## Your assignments

**My assignments** lists the routes you were given. Tap one to see its map and segments. **Blue** segments are required, **orange dashed** ones are optional, **green** ones are done, **grey** ones are skipped.

## Start a walk

On the assignment page, do the three steps in order:

1. **GPS check.** Tap **Start GPS check**. Stand still outside with a clear view of the sky. It passes when your position is steady and accurate. If it does not settle after 90 seconds you may tap **Skip GPS check**; the walk is then looked at by an administrator.
2. **Camera & storage.** Tap **Check camera & storage**. You should see the camera picture. Hold the phone so the camera faces forward.
3. **Record.** Tap **Start recording**, then walk.

## While recording

The screen shows a **map** on top and your **camera** below. You can drag the bar between them.

- The blue dot is you. The red dashed line and the arrow tell you where to go next and how far.
- The numbers at the bottom show how much of each segment is covered. Walk each required segment until it is green.
- An optional segment can be skipped with **Skip it**.
- **Keep the screen on and stay in this page.** If you switch to another app or lock the screen, **recording stops**. If that happens, start a new recording.
- The recording uploads in the background while you walk when there is signal. Poor signal is fine: it uploads later.
- To stop, tap **Stop** and tap again within three seconds.

## When you are done

Tap **Finish & check with the server**. The server checks that everything uploaded and that the required segments were covered, then shows the result:

- **completed**: all done.
- **needs review**: all covered, but an administrator will look at it.
- **in progress** with a reason: something is missing (for example a segment is not covered enough). Record again.
- **pending sync**: a recording is still uploading. Wait for a connection and tap Finish again.

Do not close or clear the browser until **On this phone** shows nothing waiting.

## If something goes wrong

| Situation | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| The page reloaded, the browser closed, or the phone restarted while recording | Open the app again and sign in. A **Recovery needed** box appears with what was saved. Tap **Finalize & upload**, then start a new recording to continue. |
| No signal | Keep walking. Recordings are saved on the phone and upload when the signal returns. |
| A message says you were signed out | The recording keeps going. Type your password in the yellow bar at the top to sign in again; uploads resume. |
| You want to sign out but data has not uploaded | The app warns you. The data stays on this phone for your account and uploads when you sign in again. |
| The phone is nearly full | Recording stops by itself when space is very low. Free space and start again. |
| It says GPS is weak | Move to open sky. Tall buildings and covered streets make GPS worse. |

Other people using the same phone cannot see or upload your data, and you cannot see theirs.
