# Worker guide

For the person walking the route with a phone. Your supervisor gives you a username and a password. You cannot change the password; if it stops working, ask them to set a new one.

The sun, moon and monitor icons at the top choose a Light, Dark or System theme. Light is often easier to read in the sun.

## Before you go

- Open the app's address in your phone's browser (Chrome on Android, Safari on iPhone) and **sign in**.
- Allow **location** and **camera** when the phone asks.
- Charge the phone. Recording uses the screen, camera and GPS for a long time.
- Free some storage. A minute of video is about 30 MB.

## Your assignments

**My assignments** lists the routes you were given. Tap one to open its home page: the map, **Start Recording**, the segments and your recordings. **Blue** segments are required, **orange dashed** ones are optional, **green** ones are done, **grey** ones are skipped. If you move the map away, **⌖ Me** brings it back to where you are and **Route** shows the whole route again.

To skip an optional segment from here, tap **Skip** beside it, type why if you like, then tap **Confirm skip**. **Undo skip** puts it back.

## Start a walk

Go outside to where you will start, then tap **Start Recording** on the assignment's home page. The checks run by themselves:

1. **GPS.** Stand still with a clear view of the sky. It passes when your position is steady and accurate, then moves on. The screen counts down the time left. If it does not settle after 90 seconds you may tap **Skip GPS check**; the walk is then looked at by an administrator. If location is blocked you cannot record or skip: allow it for this site in your browser settings and tap **Try again**.
2. **Camera & storage.** You should see the camera picture. If you do not, allow the camera and tap **Try again**. If there is not enough storage, free up space on the phone and tap **Check again**.

The **Start recording** button stays grey until both pass. Hold the phone so the camera faces forward, then tap it and walk. Once the GPS check has passed, it is not asked again for this assignment; tap **Redo** if you want to run it again.

## While recording

The screen shows a **map** on top and your **camera** below. You can drag the bar between them.

- The blue dot is you. The red dashed line and the arrow tell you where to go next and how far.
- The small arrows on the route show which way to walk each segment.
- The numbers at the bottom show how much of each segment is covered. Walk each required segment until it is green.
- An optional segment can be skipped with **Skip it**.
- **Keep the screen on and stay in this page.** If you switch to another app or lock the screen, **recording stops**. If that happens, start a new recording.
- The recording uploads in the background while you walk when there is signal. Poor signal is fine: it uploads later.
- To stop, tap **Stop** and tap again within three seconds.

## When you are done

When you stop, you are back on the assignment's home page. **Check & finish** unlocks once your recordings have uploaded and the required segments are covered; the line under the button says what it is waiting for. Tap it and the server checks everything, then shows the result:

- **completed**: all done.
- **needs review**: all covered, but an administrator will look at it. The button stays locked until they do.
- **in progress** with a reason: something is missing (for example a segment is not covered enough). Record again.
- **pending sync**: the server is missing a recording. Wait for a connection and tap the button again.

**Recordings** lists everything you recorded for this assignment, newest first, with where it is now:

- **Waiting to upload** or **Uploading**: still on this phone. Do not close or clear the browser until these are gone. Once the server has verified a recording, the phone deletes its copy.
- **✓ Saved**: safe on the server. You can download your **Video** (after the server has finished preparing it) and the **GPS (.json)**. Only your own files appear.

## If something goes wrong

| Situation | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| The page reloaded, the browser closed, or the phone restarted while recording | Open the app again and sign in. A **Recovery needed** box appears with what was saved. Tap **Finalize & upload**, then start a new recording to continue. |
| No signal | Keep walking. Recordings are saved on the phone and upload when the signal returns. |
| A message says you were signed out | The recording keeps going. Type your password in the yellow bar at the top to sign in again; uploads resume. |
| You want to sign out but data has not uploaded | The app warns you. The data stays on this phone for your account and uploads when you sign in again. |
| The phone is nearly full | Recording stops by itself when space is very low. Free space and start again. |
| Location is blocked | You cannot record. Allow location for this site in your browser settings and tap **Try again**. |
| It says GPS is weak | Move to open sky. Tall buildings and covered streets make GPS worse. |
| Assignment not found | It was withdrawn or removed, or the link is old. Go back to your list. Anything still on this phone is kept. |
| Can't reach the server | The first time you open an assignment it needs a connection. The page keeps trying by itself. |

Other people using the same phone cannot see or upload your data, and you cannot see theirs.
